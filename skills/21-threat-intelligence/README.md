# 21 — Threat Intelligence

Turning raw indicators into decisions. A feed of IPs is data; knowing which actor targets your sector and how they operate is intelligence. This domain covers collection, enrichment, and getting intel to the people who act on it.

| 03 | [actor-tracking](03-actor-tracking/SKILL.md) | Follow a threat group over time | ✅ |
| 04 | [misp-and-sharing](04-misp-and-sharing/SKILL.md) | Run a sharing platform and use STIX/TAXII | ✅ |
